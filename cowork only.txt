**Things to decide before outreach:**
- **Competitor setback:** Ultragenyx's apazunersen works the same way as obudanersen and missed its Phase 3 primary endpoint on 2 Sep 2026. That is a risk for the whole drug class. It also leaves its trial sites (Hamburg, Leipzig, LMU Munich, Parc Taulí, Necker and Marseille) experienced with spinal-injection dosing and potentially open to REVEAL's adult cohort or CHAMPION.
- **Investigator names aren't public:** no public trial registry names site leads for REVEAL or the other trials. The likely leads (for example Makowski at LMU Munich and Roche Martínez at Parc Taulí) are marked to verify. The Italian trial leads come from a single hospital press release.
- **Tier 1 centres in Germany, France and the UK:** some only qualify on a loose reading of the template's criteria. Examples are national substitutes for the European Reference Networks in Germany, and counting national trial coordination as registry coordination at Necker and Marseille. All of these are flagged.
- **Spain has no Tier 1 centre:** I found no Spanish guideline authors. Parc Taulí and Puerta de Hierro are noted as national anchors but stay at Tier 2.
- **UK:** only 8 of the 17 UK KOLs are doctors; the rest are psychologists, researchers or allied health staff. Manchester was moved to Tier 2 because I couldn't find any recent Angelman papers from there.
- **Industry links:** several KOLs work on both Ionis and competitor trials (for example D'Arrigo, Bahi-Buisson and Servais). Check their industry-payment disclosures before inviting them to an advisory board.

**Corrections to earlier assumptions about the trials:**
- REVEAL now shows as fully enrolled with 156 participants, not still recruiting adults.
- CHAMPION is registered (NCT07782827) but has US sites only.
- Rugonersen has moved from Roche to Oak Hill Bio and is in a Phase 3 trial, BEACON.
- Obudanersen does not have the EU's PRIME fast-track status; that belongs to apazunersen.
